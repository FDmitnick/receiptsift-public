FAQ

Before you upload.

What PDFs are supported?

Text-based bills and receipts work directly. Scanned pages are processed with OCR. The current limit is 20 MB and 30 pages per file.

Does ReceiptSift provide accounting advice?

No. It organizes document data. Always review extracted values before using them for accounting, tax, or reimbursement.

How long does ReceiptSift keep my files?

Single-document and batch source files, results, previews, and corrections are encrypted for up to 7 days; deleting an item or batch removes them sooner.
