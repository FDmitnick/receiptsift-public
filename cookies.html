Cookie Policy.

This Policy explains the cookies and browser storage ReceiptSift uses on its public website and protected PDF parsing application.

Current position: ReceiptSift uses one strictly necessary first-party session cookie. Optional first-party analytics run only after you choose “Allow analytics.” We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, browser fingerprints, or cross-site behavioral advertising.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text records stored by a browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can save preferences on your device without sending them automatically with every web request.

2. Technologies we use

3. Third-party requests

Pages currently request font files from Google Fonts. The font provider may receive technical request information such as IP address, browser type, and requested resource under its own privacy terms. ReceiptSift does not use that request to track users across websites.

4. Your choices

You can allow or decline optional analytics in the preference notice and change that choice on this page. You can also delete cookies and local storage through browser settings. Blocking the essential session cookie will prevent sign-in and protected app features from working. Declining analytics does not affect the product.

5. Consent and future changes

ReceiptSift displays a preference notice before optional page-view and important-click analytics start. Operational events required to create an account, enforce usage, process a payment, prevent abuse, or maintain security are part of providing the service and are not controlled by the analytics preference. We do not use the optional analytics for targeted advertising.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or browser storage may be sent to contact@example.invalid.
